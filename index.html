Part of the Pedlar family

Peko Peko Pedlar is the Pimlico shop of the Little Bread Pedlar, the bakery behind the handmade croissants and brownies on the board by the bike outside.

It’s a small shop with a big counter. Locals stop for a flat white and a pastry on the way to work, pick up a loaf for the week, and sometimes leave with a piece of pottery from the shelves.

“Quiet Pimlico bakery where locals stop for their morning coffee and pastry ritual.”

Pearl Club, London bakery guide

Good to know

Can I reserve bread?

Yes. Call 07492 930017 and we’ll put a loaf aside for you to collect.

Is there seating?

Only a little inside, so most people take away. On a nice day there may be a table outside.

Do you have plant milk?

Coconut, soy and oat, for 40p extra.

Do you take cards?

Yes, cards and contactless phone payments.
